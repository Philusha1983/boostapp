What it does

Everything you already do on the BoostApp website — automated, organized, and a little bit magical.

🎯

Sniping, done right

The extension fires the exact moment the studio's 72-hour registration window opens. Set it and forget it.

📅

Weekly slots

Tick the classes you attend every week — they're booked for you again and again, automatically.

🗓️

Three views

A list, a week grid, or a full calendar of your subscription period — including a preview of the next one.

💳

Subscription at a glance

Entries left, daily cap, validity dates — always in sync with your BoostApp account.

📊

History dashboard

A full-page dashboard of all your past classes with monthly stats, streaks and personal highlights.

🌍

Speaks your language

English, Hebrew, Russian, Ukrainian and Arabic — with full right-to-left support and a guided tour for new users.

Installed in 2 minutes

No store, no account — it's a simple folder you load into Chrome once.

Download & unzip

Hit the green button above, then unzip the file into a folder you'll keep (e.g. Documents).

Open the extensions page

In Chrome, copy chrome://extensions into the address bar and press Enter.

Turn on Developer mode

It's the small switch in the top corner of that page.

Load unpacked

Click "Load unpacked", choose the unzipped folder, and pin the icon to your toolbar. Done!

💡 Then just open BoostApp and sign in once — the extension takes it from there. A guided tour will show you around, and it checks for new versions daily and shows a banner when an update is out.

⚠️ The honest fine print

Boost Auto-Book is an independent, unofficial project. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Boost / BoostApp (Training Harmony) or your studio.

Nothing is hacked and nothing shady happens: the extension only uses the same data the BoostApp website itself shows you once you sign in, and every action it performs — registering, cancelling, viewing your history — is something you could do by hand on the website. It just does it faster, and while you sleep.

Your class history is fetched from your own profile and stored only on your computer. It is never uploaded, shared, or sent anywhere.

🔒 Your data stays yours

No servers, no analytics, no accounts. Everything the extension knows lives in your browser's local storage on your computer.

You can export it, import it on another computer, or delete the extension — and it's all gone. The code is open source, so anyone can verify this.
